How it works

Three steps. One thing at a time.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you sell.

    Type it like you would say it to a friend. We ask one smart follow-up at a time, only about what we still need to find the right buyers.

  2. 2

    Watch it do the homework.

    It searches, opens each business's own pages, checks they match your must-haves, and drops the rest. You see every step as it happens.

  3. 3

    Approve and reach out.

    Pick your buyers, edit the drafts, choose the channel. Messages go out from your own sender, on the schedule you set.
